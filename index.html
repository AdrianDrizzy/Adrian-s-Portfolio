PharmaX

Pharmacy software that keeps selling when the network drops.

A Windows system for running a retail pharmacy: the till, the dispensary, medical aid claims, stock, suppliers, debtors, the books and reports. Each till runs on its own SQLite database and syncs to MariaDB on the main computer. Its assistant, Ask PharmaX, runs a small language model on the pharmacy's own computer, so it answers how-to questions and builds reports from plain words without any data leaving the building.

PhiNine

A 24/7 trading platform built on Fibonacci ratios and Gann's Square of Nine.

A private platform for a pilot group of three traders. CoolConnect Lead Engine

Finds people whose fridge or aircon needs fixing, and asks before it calls.

A lead engine for refrigeration and air-conditioning repairs. It discovers likely customers, sends a single permission request, qualifies the people who say yes with an AI voice agent and hands booked jobs to technician partners. POPIA rules live in the code and the database: consent is asked for once and never after a refusal, and consent records cannot be edited or deleted.

  • .NET 10
  • ASP.NET Core
  • EF Core
  • Azure SQL
  • Hangfire

BethRapha

An online shop for practical guides on health, faith, family and money.

Readers preview the first pages of a guide for free, pay with EcoCash, OneMoney or card through Paynow, and download a PDF stamped with their name. It runs on ordinary PHP hosting with orders kept in SQLite, so there is no database server to look after, and it handles sign-in, promotion codes, daily backups and the admin pages itself.
